A premium, ultra-lightweight, and SEO-optimized WordPress theme designed for home decor blogs, interior design magazines, and DIY enthusiasts.

== Description ==

DecorZa is built from the ground up for the home decor niche. With zero jQuery bloat, pure vanilla JavaScript, native Schema.org JSON-LD markup, and complete WordPress Customizer integration, DecorZa ensures your blog achieves 90+ on Google PageSpeed while delivering a magazine-grade reading experience.

### Features
* **Ultra-Lightweight**: Under 500KB total size, zero runtime dependencies.
* **SEO Optimized**: Built-in Schema.org JSON-LD (WebSite, Organization, Article, Breadcrumbs, Person), Open Graph, and Twitter Cards.
* **Mobile-First Responsive**: Handcrafted for phones, tablets, and desktops with smooth slide-in drawer and sticky navigation.
* **WordPress Customizer API**: Control typography, colors, homepage sections, reading view, footer, and social links live.
* **Dark / Light Mode**: Instant toggle with local storage persistence.
* **Built-in Tools**: Automatic Table of Contents generator, estimated reading time, Pinterest-optimized floating social share, and related posts.
* **Custom Widgets**: 5 niche widgets including About, Popular Posts, Categories with Badges, Author Profile, and Newsletter.

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin dashboard, navigate to **Appearance > Themes**.
2. Click **Add New** then **Upload Theme**.
3. Select the `decorza.zip` archive and click **Install Now**.
4. Click **Activate** to make DecorZa your active theme.
5. Go to **Appearance > Customize** to configure your logo, colors, hero banner, and homepage sections.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does DecorZa require any page builders? =
No. DecorZa is completely self-contained and does not require Elementor, WPBakery, or any external page builder. All homepage sections and styling are managed via the WordPress Customizer.

= Does DecorZa use jQuery? =
No. All UI interactions (mobile navigation drawer, sticky header, search modal, dark mode toggle, smooth scrolling, and TOC) run on Vanilla JavaScript.

= How do I customize colors and typography? =
Navigate to **Appearance > Customize > DecorZa Theme Settings > General Settings** to select your preferred primary, secondary, and accent colors, as well as Google Fonts (Playfair Display and Inter).
